Dinner Decided

Weekly dinner plans and shopping lists for busy parents

What it does

Dinner Decided takes a public web page link and family dietary preferences to generate a seven-day dinner schedule and an aggregated ingredient list.

Who it is for

This tool helps single working mothers with young children create simple weekly meal plans without storing their personal data.

What it does not do

  • It does not promise specific cooking results or income.
  • It does not store user inputs or past results.
  • It does not require accounts or passwords.
  • It gives no financial, legal or health advice and promises no result.
